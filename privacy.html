Sumurai

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Sumurai ("the app") is a mental-maths game made by Aakriti Ahuja. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: there are no accounts and no ads, your progress stays on your phone, and we use anonymous analytics to understand how the app is used so we can improve it.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress (levels, stars, scores, streaks, settings and your invite code) is stored only on your device. We can't see it, and it's deleted if you uninstall the app.

Analytics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to see, in aggregate, how people use Sumurai, for example which levels are completed and which game modes are played. Firebase collects:

  • events about how you use the app, such as starting a level or sharing a result
  • an app-instance identifier created by Firebase, which isn't linked to your name or contact details
  • basic device information (model, OS version, language) and an approximate location at the country or region level, which Firebase derives from your IP address

We don't collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We don't use the advertising ID and we don't track you across other apps or websites. We don't sell your data or share it with anyone other than Google as our analytics provider. Google processes this data under its own privacy terms: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.

Sharing

When you choose to share an achievement, the app creates an image and message on your phone and hands them to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp, through your phone's share menu. We don't see what you share or who you share it with. Shared links include your anonymous invite code so we can count how many people joined through shares.

Children

Sumurai is intended for users aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can stop all analytics by uninstalling the app. Reinstalling creates a new, unrelated identifier.
  • To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app instance, email us with the date you installed the app and your invite code (shown on the Progress screen).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
