Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Sumurai ("the app") is a mental-maths game made by Aakriti Ahuja ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends, and the choices you have. It applies to the Sumurai apps for iPhone and Android.

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases.
  • Your progress and your first name stay on your phone, with a backup in your own iCloud or Google account so you don't lose them. We can't see either.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to count, anonymously, how the app is used, so we can make it better. No name, email or advertising ID is sent, and we don't track you across other apps.
  • We never sell your data.

What the app keeps on your phone

Everything you do in Sumurai is saved on your device:

  • Your progress: levels and stars, scores, brain speed and belt, tricks learned, your streak and the days you played.
  • Your first name, if you type one when you start. It's only used to greet you inside the app.
  • Your settings: sound, vibration, daily goal and reminder time.
  • An invite code: a short random code (for example K7Q2XM) that the app adds to links when you share a result. It isn't linked to who you are.

We don't receive any of this. It's never sent to us or to analytics.

Backup to your own iCloud or Google account

So that a new phone or a reinstall doesn't wipe your progress, the app lets your phone back it up to your own account:

  • iPhone: your progress, name and settings are saved to your iCloud using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, if you're signed in to iCloud. When you reinstall the app or use a new iPhone with the same Apple Account, they come back automatically.
  • Android: your progress, name and settings are included in your phone's Google backup (Android Auto Backup), if backup is turned on. They come back when you reinstall or set up a new phone with the same Google account.

These backups are stored by Apple or Google in your account, under their privacy policies. We can't access them. You can turn them off in your phone's iCloud or backup settings.

Daily reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks your permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no server is involved and no data is sent when they appear. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in total across all players, how Sumurai is used: for example which levels are hard, which game modes are popular and whether the tour helps. Firebase collects:

WhatExamples
App eventsopening the app, starting or finishing a level (with its number, stars and time), finishing the tour, changing a setting (which setting, not your name), tapping Share
Simple player traitswhich path you're on, your belt, a streak range such as "3–6 days", your daily goal and whether reminders are on
An app-instance IDa random identifier created by Firebase for this install. It isn't linked to your name, email or phone number, and it changes if you reinstall the app.
Device informationphone model, operating system version, language and app version
Approximate locationcountry, region or city, which Google works out from your internet connection's IP address. The app never asks for or uses GPS.

We don't collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The app doesn't use the advertising ID (IDFA on iPhone, AAID on Android), doesn't show ads and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites. On iPhone, Apple's App Privacy section for Sumurai lists the same data: device ID, product interaction and coarse location, used for analytics only, not linked to you and not used for tracking.

Google processes this data for us as our analytics provider, under its terms: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months and is then deleted automatically. All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Sharing

When you choose to share a result or your streak, the app makes a picture and a short message on your phone and hands them to the app you pick (WhatsApp, Messages and so on) through your phone's share menu. We don't see what you share or who you share it with. The link in the message, and the picture, include your anonymous invite code, so we can count how many people joined through shares.

Children

Sumurai is a general-audience app designed for teens and adults, and it isn't directed at children under 13. It has no accounts, chat, ads or purchases, and it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you're a parent or guardian and think your child has sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Erase your progress: in the app, open Settings and choose Reset progress. This clears it on your phone and replaces the copy in your iCloud (on iPhone). Deleting the app also removes everything stored on the phone.
  • Turn off backup: switch off iCloud or Google backup for the app in your phone's settings.
  • Stop analytics: uninstall the app. Reinstalling creates a new, unrelated app-instance ID.
  • Ask us to delete analytics data: email us with your invite code (it's printed at the bottom of any result picture you've shared) or the approximate date you installed the app, and we'll ask Google to delete the matching data.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we don't hold your name or contact details, the steps above cover these rights; email us if you need anything else.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also mention it in the app's update notes.
